# Leave a resumable handoff

Record enough current evidence for another worker to resume without reconstructing the conversation.

Include the task objective and acceptance, repository/revision/dirty state, ownership and granted scope, changed artifacts, and actual checks with inputs, environment, outcome and timestamp. Distinguish independent-review findings from unresolved risks and checks not run.

State accepted decisions, remaining blockers and the next executable action with its prerequisites. Preserve failed attempts that explain the current state. Reference existing canonical policy and project evidence rather than copying private conversations or protected data.

A next step is a recommendation within the recorded authority, not an extension of scope or budget. If a human gate remains, name it explicitly; do not mark blocked or exhausted work successful. Keep the handoff concise and update stale claims when its underlying artifacts change.
